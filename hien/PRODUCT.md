# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
One reader: Hiền, Tinh's girlfriend, opening a link on her birthday — most likely on her phone, possibly again later on a laptop. She is not completing a task; she is being given a gift and should feel personally known by it.

## Product Purpose
A virtual birthday card (2026) from Tinh to Hiền. Success is her smiling, reading every letter, and wanting to come back to it. It is a keepsake, not a site.

## Positioning
Written by one person for one person: the private names ("my rabbit", "my em bé"), the shared details (strawberries, flowers, little adventures and dates), and the bear-kiss GIF are things no generic e-card could carry.

## Operating Context
Shared as a link (tinhnguyen20.github.io/hien/), opened on a phone first. Static GitHub Pages hosting; no build step, no backend.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static HTML/CSS/JS only, served from GitHub Pages. Third-party scripts from CDNs are acceptable (anime.js is already used).
- Every typeface must render Vietnamese diacritics correctly ("Hiền", "em bé").
- She should reach the letters quickly: no long intro gate.
- Photo section ships with placeholders now; Tinh will add real photos to `hien/assets/` later, so dropping one in must be trivial.

## Brand Commitments
- Tone: polished and elevated, but cute. It must never look homemade or like clip-art (the round-1 flat, hand-drawn dollhouse was rejected as "a kid's first HTML"), and it should not feel stiff or like a wedding invite.
- Imagery stays minimal and sparing: refined motifs used like brand marks, no busy illustration.
- Motion should be gentle; no confetti overload.
- Personal motifs to carry: rabbit/bunny, carrot, strawberries, flowers, and the bear-kiss GIF as a featured moment.

## Evidence on Hand
- Four letters of copy in `hien/index.html` (verbatim, written by Tinh; do not rewrite).
- `hien/assets/bear-kiss-bear-kisses.gif`.
- No photos yet — never fabricate them; placeholders only.

## Product Principles
1. Her name and his words lead; decoration serves them.
2. Every interaction should feel like opening something.
3. Cute, not childish; playful, not noisy.
4. Phone-first: one thumb, portrait, no precision targets.
