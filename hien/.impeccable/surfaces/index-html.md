# Surface: Hiền's birthday card (hien/index.html)

Mode: Experience. One reader (Hiền), phone-first, opened from a shared link on her birthday.
Job: open four letters from Tinh, see the bear keepsake and the photos of them, end on the birthday wish.
Constraints: letter copy verbatim; Vietnamese-capable faces; no long intro; gentle motion; photos are placeholders until Tinh adds files.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A boutique letter set laid out on a strawberry-red desk: polished, elevated, and cute in the small details. It refuses the hand-drawn clip-art dollhouse (round 1, rejected as "a kid's first HTML") and the generic pink e-card.

OWN-WORLD: Strawberry-red ground (#c93a4d) with fine grain. Four tinted paper envelopes (blush, sage, butter, powder) with printed liners, embossed wax seals in matching deep tones, and cotton letter paper in berry ink. Prata for display, Imperial Script for her name, Petrona for the letters, Be Vietnam Pro for microcopy. Imagery is sparing: small embossed motifs only (bunny, flower, carrot, sparkle).

STORY: She reads "Happy Birthday, Hiền", breaks the first seal, and the letter rises out of its envelope and unfolds. Scrolling, she opens three more, finds the bear keepsake taped down, shuffles the photo stack, and the last letter releases a few paper hearts.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Big cream Prata "Happy Birthday," with "Hiền" written in script beneath, and the blush envelope with its red bunny seal below, with "tap the seal to open" under it.

FORM: Luxe stationery set (user-pinned in round 2, over the seed's re-roll); prior seed key 0c412eb9 retired. Signature interaction: seal → flap → the letter rises and unfolds on its fold. Motion grammar: paper physics (lift, settle, fold); one ease-out curve and one soft spring.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
